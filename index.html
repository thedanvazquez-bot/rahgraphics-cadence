Invoice Follow-Up Cadence
Design is the evidence. Strategy is the advantage.
Channels Email SMS Phone Human review required Final / escalation Internal only
Cadence Flow
Stage 0
Invoice Sent
Day 0
EMAIL
Establish record. Send invoice + project summary + payment link.
Tone: neutral, professional
Stage 1
Pre-Due Reminder
Day -3
EMAIL
Helpful heads-up. Remove friction early (PO, W-9, remit-to).
Tone: warm, helpful
Stage 2
Due-Date Reminder
Day 0
EMAIL
Same-day prompt. Make payment path obvious.
Tone: friendly, brief
Stage 3
First Overdue
Day +3
EMAIL
Assume oversight. Ask for the blocker (AP queue, PO, question).
Tone: polite, assumes oversight
Stage 4
Firm Follow-Up
Day +7
EMAILSMS
Ask for a payment date. Three numbered options to make reply easy.
Tone: direct, asks for date
Stage 5
Manager Escalation
Day +14
EMAILPHONE
Reference prior reminders and Net 30 terms. Two clean paths forward.
Tone: firm — human review required
Stage 6
Formal Final Notice
Day +30
EMAILSMSPHONE
State the +5 day deadline. Pause new work if silent.
Tone: formal — human review required
Stage 7
Pre-Collections Demand
Day +45
EMAILCERT MAIL
Formal demand. State consequence and costs.
Tone: final demand — human review required

Stage 8 — Internal Collections Review (Day +60)

No customer email at this stage. Decision task only: refer to collections agency, write off the balance, or hold. New project work paused until cleared.

Pause Triggers — Stop the Cadence

If the customer replies with any of these, set the row's Pause Status and switch to the matching exception template. Do not keep sending standard reminders alongside an exception flow.
Dispute
Use Dispute / Resolution template. Ask for line item + concern. Reply within 2 business days.
Missing PO
Use AP Process / PO Required template. Reissue invoice with PO ref.
Promised Date
Use Payment Promise Confirmation. Single follow-up at the promised date only.
Partial Payment
Use Partial Payment / Balance. Re-enter cadence for remaining balance.
Wrong Contact
Forward to correct AP contact. Log new contact in Notes column.
